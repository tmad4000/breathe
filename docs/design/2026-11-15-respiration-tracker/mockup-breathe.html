What this shows

  • Modes are tabs before Start and while paused. While running they fold into one title button, so a stray tap cannot switch mode; tap it to open the list. "Respiration" is renamed Respiration Tracker.
  • Tracking message, two sizes. In a mode that cannot work without tracking (Respiration Tracker, Coherence) the message is a large amber block with the fix on the second line, and the number dims. In Coach, which keeps guiding, it is a small pill. Switch to Coach with tracking lost to compare.
  • Three on-screen chips above Start/Pause/Stop: pace (or pattern in Coach and Guide), Guide tone, Detection tone. Guide tone is the "timer sound". Detection tone plays a short tick when the app detects your breath turning in (higher) or out (lower), and the ring flashes, so detection can be checked with sound off too. Turn it on and press Running.
  • Switching mid-session. Pattern or pace: same session, applied at the next breath, with Undo. Mode: the current session is saved and a new one continues in the new mode (running stays running; paused opens the new mode ready to Start).
  • Debug recording is a dot, top right before the sound and camera buttons: a 6 px dot and 11 px low-contrast text, with elapsed time while a session records. Tap for what is saved, the off switch and Mark. The first-run notice is one line in a toast, not a card. Version text stays top left by the gear.
