## Others (from general knowledge, not a fresh review)

- RESPeRATE / 2breathe: chest belt measures the breath; tones start at the
  person's own rhythm and slowly lengthen the exhale. Closest precedent for
  match-then-lead.
- Breathing Zone: microphone check sets a starting rate, then eases to a target.
- HeartMath Inner Balance: ear or finger sensor, live coherence score, pacer is
  adjustable but does not adapt.
- Polar Serene: fixed 6/min with a simple three-zone "in sync" readout.
- Apple Watch Mindfulness: one visual, fixed rate, wrist taps.
- Breathwrk, Othership, Oak: pattern libraries grouped by goal; no sensing.
